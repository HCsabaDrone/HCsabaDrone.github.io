03 / A little context

Good work starts
with looking closer.

Drones and aerial imagery are currently part of my hobby, but I would like to make a living from it one day. This website is the first step on that journey: a place to share what I see and what I can create.

I do not have a big production team or a polished story behind me. I bring my curiosity, creativity, willingness to learn, and all the knowledge I have gathered so far.
